Email lova@threeohfivestudios.com for help with Eventi. If something isn’t working, include what you were trying to do and the city you were browsing.

Browse without sharing your location

Tap the area name at the top of the map and select Los Angeles, New York, or Chicago. You can switch cities at any time. To find listings near you, open the area picker and tap Use My Location.

Sign in with Apple

Tap the account icon at the top right, then tap Sign in with Apple and follow the iPhone prompts. You can browse as a guest. Sign in if you want to bookmark events.

Delete your account

While signed in, tap the account icon, choose Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your Eventi account and bookmarks.

If Eventi asks you to finish removing Apple access, go to iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Sign in with Apple. Select Eventi and tap Delete. Apple’s instructions
